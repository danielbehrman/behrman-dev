# behrman.dev

Personal home base for Dan Behrman — mechanical engineer, Disney Imagineer,
builder, writer, and dad.

This is where Shipyard lives. Shipyard is the studio where I turn the things
I can't stop thinking about into real products, stories, and tools that
(hopefully) make people's lives a little better.

---

## What's here

A single-page portfolio and project showcase built with Next.js and hosted
on Vercel. No CMS, no bloat — just fast, clean, and easy to maintain.

### Projects in the Shipyard

**Covered** — Web App *(Coming Soon)*
PTO and family calendar planner. Built because school days off have a way
of sneaking up on you.

**Liftoff Lab** — iOS Game *(Coming Soon)*
A physics-based rocket builder game for kids, co-designed with my 7-year-old
son Ari. Real physics. Real fun.

**Dragons Don't Eat Ice Cream** — Children's Book *(In Progress)*
An anaphylaxis awareness story for kids with food allergies and the parents
who love them. Written because my son Joshy deserves to see himself in a book
and not feel like the odd one out at the lunch table.
